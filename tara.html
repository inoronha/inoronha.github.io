TARA: Non-contact Patient Registration and Monitoring System

Contactless patient registration with offline and online speech recognition for regional languages. Led the end-to-end development at the Robotics and Control Lab, IIT Roorkee, and transitioned it from prototype to product, deployed at a hospital. The work led to the founding of Robolife Ltd., a faculty enterprise valued at $800K. IN 479029, granted Dec 2023.
